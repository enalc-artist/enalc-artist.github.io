Recovered readings of the work

Four points of entry into what is taking shape.

Working curatorial texts developed in previous conversations. They are not presented as published criticism, nor do they replace a text signed by a curator.

01

Reading of Cosmic Face · 2026

Inner Constellation

The face ceases to be a portrait and becomes a nervous cartography. Particles travel along its lines like impulses; eyes, cheeks and lips pulse as an almost imperceptible breath keeps the image alive.

02

Reading of Cosmic Face · 2026

Magnetic Face

Identity appears as a gravitational field. Planes of depth, liquid neon and drifting nebulae position the eyes and mouth as centres of attraction within a presence that never fully settles.

03

Reading of Geometric Composition

Breathing Architecture

Geometry sheds its stillness and becomes a score. Columns, turquoise waves and violet planes move out of step, like an architecture rehearsing its own breath.

04

Reading of Geometric Composition

Submerged Temple

Three depths, watery reflections and mineral refractions turn the plane into space. The work does not illustrate a temple: it produces the physical sensation of advancing towards a submerged structure.
