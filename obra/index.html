Urban Décollage Series

The work emerges from fragments of advertisements stripped from city walls. Enrique Alcides retrieves these superimposed layers, brings them into the studio, intervenes in the material through painting and reworks the result digitally. The wall functions simultaneously as archive, support and collective memory.

Repetition · difference · visual populations

One image becomes many.

These series examine how an image can remain recognisable while colour, rhythm and internal structure keep changing.

Generative image series · 1,000 variations

Mis Monstruitos Favoritos — ENALC 1000

The project begins with a little monster Enrique Alcides drew as a child. Years later, he digitised it and produced more than seventy colour variants by hand. A generative system then extended the same figure into a population of one thousand, exploring how repetition can preserve an identity while difference continually unsettles it.

Origin
Childhood drawing by the artist
Process
Digitisation, manual colour variation and generative system
Scope
1,000 variations
