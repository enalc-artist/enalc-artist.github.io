Repetition · difference · visual populations

One image becomes many.

These series examine how an image can remain recognisable while colour, rhythm and internal structure keep changing.

Modular colour study · 30 variations

My Favorite Baby Mozta

The project begins with a little monster Enrique Alcides drew as a child while living in Western Sahara. Rediscovered and re-signified in adulthood, it was digitised, and the first recovered originals were coloured by hand. A generative system then extended the same figure into a population of variations, exploring how repetition can preserve an identity while difference continually unsettles it. Initially conceived as a possible NFT series, the project may later give movement to the little monsters through animation.

Thirty variations explore a six-zone colour system. Background, long hair, long tie, body fur, belly and eyes/iris change independently, allowing the same figure to hold multiple chromatic identities.

Structure
Six independently variable colour zones
