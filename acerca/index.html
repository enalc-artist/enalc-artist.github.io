Biography / statement

Enrique Alcides works between images.

His practice brings together painting, drawing, digital creation, performance and audiovisual research. The body, memory and the unstable construction of identity run through a practice now expanding into generative systems and immersive installations.

OriginLas Palmas de Gran Canaria

Based inMadrid

PracticePainting · digital · installation

SignatureENALC

Born in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria and shaped by life between the Sahara, Cádiz and Madrid, Enrique Alcides has developed a visual practice informed by his long experience in film and on stage. This dual perspective—constructing an image and inhabiting it—positions the body as measure, archive and surface of transformation.

He has participated in video installation and multidisciplinary projects, and his visual work has featured in group exhibitions in Madrid. His current research explores the passage between painterly matter, code, light and immersive space.

Artistic statement · working version

Every identity is an image in progress.

I work with images that remember, mutate and contradict their appearance. I am interested in the moment when a figure can no longer be explained through a single reading: when the face becomes landscape, colour adopts a behaviour, matter seems to breathe, or the viewer discovers that they, too, occupy the interior of the work.

Painting remains a physical root, but no longer a limit. Code, projection, movement and installation allow me to turn a visual intuition into a living system. I do not seek to illustrate a future; I prefer to create the conditions in which several futures can be felt at once.
