Artist residency proposal · JRC, European Commission · Decoding Depression · Ispra, 2027

E la nave va

A ship drawn in section. What weighs on a life is stowed below the waterline; what sustains it floats above. The waterline rises and falls with anonymous contributions and with the currents that move the whole fleet at once: economic crises, pandemics, wars, climate.

On the hull sits a Plimsoll line, the safe-load mark that British law made compulsory in 1876. Political decisions are not the sea: they are the rule that sets how much load is safe. The interactive fleet shows six ships (school, work, family, leisure, relationships, digital access), live statistics by age, situation and disability, and two scenarios for 2040. All data in the prototype are invented.

Artist residency proposal · Medialab Matadero · Situated Research · Madrid, 2026

In the Shade. The Art of Making Room

I want to investigate what makes a place welcome us. I begin with an everyday gesture: looking for shade. Across six sites in the ecosystem around Matadero Madrid, I will walk, listen, photograph and compose with social organisations, workers, students, neighbours and people who pass through the area.

The shadow is both a material reality and an image of care: a place that lets another body stop, rest and feel part of the world, without having to buy, produce or justify its presence.

Three Lands for a Single Present

The Image That Never Existed
