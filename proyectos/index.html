Proto-Ideas Laboratory

Projects before they become inevitable.

These are not vague promises: each proto-idea contains a question, a spatial form, a technical development path and a concrete opening for collaboration.

P—01

Immersive installation · open research

Three Lands for a Single Present

Three projected territories coexist and bleed into one another across a central sphere, the walls, floor and ceiling. The experience asks the audience not to contemplate possible futures from the outside, but to move through them.

Seeking: technical residency · co-producing institution · multichannel design

P—02

Generative art · photonics · expanded portraiture

The Image That Never Existed

A face emerges as a magnetic field: neon lines, planes of light and constellations construct and dissolve it in real time. Identity is not a fixed form but an interference.

Seeking: media lab · scientific collaboration · projection space

P—03

Moving image · body · transformation

The Metamorphosis of Sated Identity

An androgynous figure advances towards the camera while body, face, clothing and landscape follow different rhythms of mutation. An inquiry into the self as reprogrammable matter.

Seeking: creative incubator · animation · digital exhibition

Images in development.
